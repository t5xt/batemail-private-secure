FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. There are no paid plans or ads.

How do I log in?

Type your email address and password. Keep both private, since anyone who has them can open your mailbox.

Where is my mail stored?

On the BateMail server, in an encrypted file only the owner can open. Mail is never deleted, and you can log in from any device.

Can I send email?

No. BateMail is receive-only. Anyone can email your address, including Gmail and Outlook users, but you can’t send mail from it.

Can I recover a lost account?

No. If you forget your address or password, the mailbox can’t be recovered.
