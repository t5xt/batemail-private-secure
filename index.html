FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. There are no paid plans or ads.

How do I log in?

Type your email address and password. Keep both private, since anyone who has them can open your mailbox.

Where is my mail stored?

On the BateMail server. Check the service’s actual storage and encryption implementation for details. Keep your credentials private.

Can I send email?

No. BateMail is receive-only. You can receive messages, but you can’t send mail from it.

Can I recover a lost account?

If you forget your address or password, account recovery may not be available. Keep your login details safe.
